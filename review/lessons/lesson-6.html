学会过去时 was / were，能说清「昨天怎么样」

← 返回看板

复习

  1. 第 5 课时态辨析（选填 She usually ___ English in the morning）：正确。studies，usually 是习惯标志词。
  2. 第 3 课物主代词（翻译：他们的学校很大）：作答 Theri school is very big。拼写错误：their 的字母顺序写反了。正确句：Their school is very big.
  3. 错词逗号连句（改错 I drink water every day, I need sleep every night）：正确。用句号断开。第 3 次错后第一次答对 → 1。
  4. 错词 we 配 are（改错 I am very busy → 我们很忙）：正确。We are very busy. 第 1 次答对 → 1。
  5. 错词句首大写（改错 those are their bags）：正确。Those are their bags. 第 1 次答对 → 1。

今日语法

讲昨天、上次、以前的事，be 动词要换成过去式：

现在 过去
I am busy. I was busy.
He / She / It is busy. He / She / It was busy.
You / We / They are busy. You / We / They were busy.

口诀：am / is → was，are → were。

  • 否定：was / were 后加 not → I was not busy. ／ They weren't here.
  • 疑问：把 was / were 提到主语前面 → Was he tired? ／ Were you at school?
  • 时间标志词：yesterday、last night、last week、two days ago

作业

  1. 选填：I ___ tired yesterday.（am / was / were）
看答案was
2. 选填：They ___ at home last night.（was / were）
看答案were
3. 改错：He were late this morning.
看答案He was late this morning.
4. 翻译：我昨天很忙。
看答案I was busy yesterday.
5. 开放题：写 5 句讲你昨天的一天，每句都要用 was 或 were，至少 1 句否定、1 句疑问。

批改

补漏块 3：

  1. 正确。What 问名字。
  2. 正确。Who 问人。
  3. 正确。Where is your school? 句尾问号别丢。

作业：

  1. 正确。yesterday 是过去时间，用 was。
  2. 正确。They 用 were。
  3. 正确。He 配 was。
  4. 正确。I was busy yesterday.
  5. 五处问题。
  • 语法（时态混用）：Tom and I were playing game 用了进行时，但这句在讲「昨天某个时刻正在做」，进行时本身可以（was / were + -ing 是过去进行时，第 7 课才正式讲），问题在 game 单数裸用 → games。
  • 语法（介词）：at yesterday 连用两次。yesterday 前面不加 at，直接说 yesterday 或 at 8 p.m. yesterday。正确：Tom and I were playing games at 8 p.m. yesterday.
  • 拼写（不规则动词）：teached → taught。teach 的过去式是不规则变化。
  • 语法（介词）：I taught my friend to use AI at yesterday → 去掉 at，或写成 yesterday evening。
  • 用词（疑问句）：What were you yesterday. 想问「你昨天怎么样」，英语用 How：How were you yesterday? 而且句尾必须是问号。
  • 任务完成度：5 句里只有 2 个不同句式（was busy / were playing），否定句没写。补一句：I was not happy yesterday.（或 I wasn't tired yesterday.）

完整改后版：I was busy yesterday. Tom and I were playing games at 8 p.m. We had a good time. I taught my friend to use AI. How were you yesterday? I was not very happy yesterday.
