第 1 篇 · Yesterday

级别：Level 2 ｜ 来源：自编

Yesterday was a busy day for me. I was at work from nine to six.

昨天对我来说是忙碌的一天。我从九点工作到六点。

At night, I was at home. I was tired, but I was happy.

晚上我在家。我很累，但很开心。

Were you busy yesterday? I was not busy last week, but this week is different.

你昨天忙吗？我上周不忙，但这周不一样了。

生词注释

busy 忙的 / from ... to ... 从……到…… / at night 在晚上 / different 不同的 / last week 上周

理解题

  1. Where was the writer at night?
看答案He was at home.
2. Was the writer busy last week?
看答案No, he wasn't.

第 2 篇 · Tom's Bad Day

看答案No, she wasn't.
