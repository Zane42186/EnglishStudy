第 1 篇 · My Day

级别：Level 1 ｜ 来源：自编

I like music. I study English every day.

我喜欢音乐。我每天学英语。

I work in a small company. I play games after work.

我在一家小公司上班。下班后我打游戏。

I need help with my English.

我的英语需要有人帮我。

生词注释

every day 每天 / company 公司 / after work 下班后 / play games 打游戏 / with 在……方面

理解题

  1. What does the writer study every day?
看答案English.
2. What does the writer do after work?
看答案He plays games.

第 2 篇 · My Teacher and I

级别：Level 1 ｜ 来源：自编

I am a student. I study English every day.

我是学生。我每天学英语。

My teacher is Amy. She is kind and patient.

我的老师是 Amy。她又和蔼又有耐心。

We are busy after work. I am tired, but I am happy.

我们下班后很忙。我很累，但我很开心。

生词注释

kind 和蔼的 / patient 有耐心的 / but 但是 / every day 每天 / after work 下班后

理解题

  1. What is Amy like?
看答案She is kind and patient.
2. Is the writer happy?
看答案Yes, he is tired but happy.
